Napa 500mg

Square Pharmaceuticals PLC

Generic Name Paracetamol
Unit Price ৳ 1.20 / tablet
Form Tablet

Indications / Uses

Fever, headache, toothache, body pain, flu symptoms, and rheumatoid arthritis pain.

Dosage & Administration

Adults: 1-2 tablets every 4-6 hours up to maximum 4000mg per day. Children: As directed by a registered physician.

Side Effects & Warnings

Rare allergic skin reactions. Excessive dosage can lead to liver damage. Avoid chronic heavy alcohol use with paracetamol.
